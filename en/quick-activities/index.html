THREE INVITATIONS · NO SIGN-UP

Three gentle activities to start at your own pace.

Choose what feels right today: a shared-interest bingo, a quiet pause or an invented scene. They run in this browser and do not save answers.

Choose an activity ↓

01 · GENTLE · 5–10 MIN

Connection bingo

Nine easy prompts for finding common ground without turning any answer into an obligation.

  1. Take turns reading the squares and choose a prompt that feels interesting.
  2. Mark it only if both people want to share something about it.
  3. A line can start a conversation; there is no need to fill the board.

Mark only the prompts you both feel like discussing. Passing is fine.

0 of 9 squares marked.

02 · WELLBEING · 2 MIN

A pause for two

A little space to slow down and decide what would feel good now. Talking, being quiet or stopping early are equally valid choices.

  1. Choose whether you want to talk, sit quietly or simply look around.
  2. Start the optional two-minute timer; breathe normally, with no pattern to follow.
  3. When it ends, each person may share one word or leave it there.

Optional timer · no sound

Ready when you are.

03 · IMAGINATION · 10 MIN

Scene workshop

Combine three everyday clues to invent a completely fictional scene. There is no need to act or share personal experiences.

  1. Generate a setting, a mood and an object.
  2. Invent a short scene: narrate it, describe it in a few words or keep it to one sentence.
  3. If a clue does not fit, change it or generate another; anyone can pass or stop.
